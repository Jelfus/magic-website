Meet Noah

Young, curious, and seriously magical.

I'm Noah, a 16-year-old close-up magician and member of the Young Magicians' Club - the youth initiative of the Magic Circle. My magic happens in your hands, around your table, and in the moments you least expect it.

Whether I'm performing for a small group or moving through a busy event, I tailor each performance to the people in front of me. The result is less a show, more a story your guests get to tell.
