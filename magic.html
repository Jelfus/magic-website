Moments people remember.

The magic is designed for real conversations and real celebrations. There is no stage to find and no audience to arrange: Noah moves naturally through the room, performing for small groups and making each person part of the impossible moment.

From a borrowed ring appearing somewhere unexpected to a prediction that seems to know exactly what someone was thinking, every routine is selected for the people in front of him. The pace can be lively, elegant or quietly astonishing, depending on the atmosphere you want to create.

01 / PRIVATE PARTIES

Turn a room of guests into one shared secret.

Private party magic gives guests an easy reason to talk to one another. Noah can move between groups during drinks, dinner or a relaxed celebration, bringing impossible moments directly to each circle. The performance is informal, interactive and flexible enough to fit the rhythm of your evening.

It is ideal for birthdays, anniversaries, garden parties and family celebrations where you want entertainment that feels personal rather than programmed.

02 / WEDDINGS

Fill the in-between moments with wonder.

Wedding days naturally have moments when guests are waiting: between the ceremony and photographs, while the room is being prepared, or before the wedding breakfast begins. Close-up magic keeps the energy warm and gives friends and family a memorable way to meet.
